# Limitations of the corrected investigation

The conclusions compare specified response rules on synthetic WS, BA and ER
networks; they are not forecasts or calibrated intervention recommendations.
Degree-aware cost weights cautious nodes by their contacts, while mean-degree
cost is retained as a sensitivity comparison. Both count protective effort
until disease extinction and omit later residual caution. Steps are not days.

The fixed trigger, fixed top-10% targeting pool and different effective decay
under imitation limit generality. Equal expected recipients per triggered
update do not imply equal unique recipients or equal cumulative costs.
Wave counts depend on the detector; the coefficient of variation does not
establish overshoot, so H3 remains unresolved. The beta sweep compares only
observation and no behaviour. Grid-average rankings depend on grid weights.
Bootstrap intervals capture seed variability within that design rather than
uncertainty in model assumptions. The ODE is a reference approximation; the
contributions of clustering and discrete time to its gap have not been isolated.
